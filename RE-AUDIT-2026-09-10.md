## The "56 URLs"

The 56 is the **`Discovered – currently not indexed`** row of the Coverage
report — Google knows these URLs but has never spent crawl budget on them.

The Coverage zip supplied contains only the per-issue *summary*
([`exports/gsc-coverage-critical-2026-09-10.csv`](exports/gsc-coverage-critical-2026-09-10.csv)),
not the URLs. To get the actual 56: **Search Console → Indexing → Pages →
click the `Discovered – currently not indexed` row → Export** (that view lists
up to 1,000 example URLs; 56 fits comfortably).

Until that export exists, the closest evidence-backed substitute is
[`exports/discovered-not-indexed-candidates.csv`](exports/discovered-not-indexed-candidates.csv)
— every indexable sitemap URL ranked by internal inbound links ascending,
because link starvation is what puts a URL in this bucket. **30 sitemap URLs
have zero internal links pointing at them**, which is the single strongest
predictor here and is very likely most of the 56.

## Two things worth reacting to before anything else

**1. Indexing has been declining for three months, not holding steady.**

| Date | Indexed | Not indexed |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-06-11 | 273 | 349 |
| 2026-07-10 | 341 | 853 |
| 2026-08-10 | 286 | 941 |
| 2026-09-03 | **285** | **1,006** |

Not-indexed has tripled while indexed drifted down. The HTTPS report shows the
same shape: 145 HTTPS URLs known on 2026-07-05, 26 by 2026-09-04.

**2. The only page earning search traffic is on the legacy host.**

Three months of Performance data is 1 click and 24 impressions, and
[`exports/gsc-performance-pages-2026-09-10.csv`](exports/gsc-performance-pages-2026-09-10.csv)
puts 22 of those 24 impressions plus the single click on
`shop.oligopolypeptides.com/products/selank-5mg-research-peptide/`.

Verified live today, and this part is **healthy**:

```
shop.oligopolypeptides.com/products/selank-5mg-research-peptide/
  →301→ www.oligopolypeptides.com/products/selank-5mg-research-peptide/  [200]
shop.oligopolypeptides.com/  →301→  www.oligopolypeptides.com/  [200]
```

One hop, correct destination, and the www target — a **404 during the August
audit** — now returns 200. The migration is wired correctly; it simply has not
been recrawled and reattributed yet. These are the first URLs to inspect.

## Canonicalisation: healthier than the buckets suggest

Checking every indexable 200 page that claims canonical status (self-canonical
or no canonical) for a shared `<title>`:

**Zero competing self-canonical pages.** `canonical-conflicts.csv` is empty and
that is the correct result. Every duplicate-title cluster resolves to one
parent already:

| Cluster | Variants | All canonical to |
|---|---:|---|
| `/compare/?division=…&compare_product=…` | 39 | `/compare/` |
| `/research-library/?record=N` | 8 | `/research-library/` |
| `/research-catalog/?opl_offer=1` | 1 | `/research-catalog/` |

That is what `Alternate page with proper canonical tag: 83` is made of — it is
working as intended, not a defect. It does spend crawl budget, which matters on
a property already struggling to get pages crawled.

**August finding #5 is fixed:** `/research-products/` now 301s to
`/research-catalog/`, exactly as recommended.

The `Duplicate, Google chose different canonical than user: 6` rows cannot be
identified from this crawl — nothing on the site declares a conflicting
canonical, so Google is overriding on content similarity alone. **Those 6 URLs
must come from the GSC export**; they are the other must-inspect set.

## Method

```
robots.txt + sitemap_index.xml → 5 child sitemaps → 234 URLs
  ↓ fetch each without auto-redirect, recording the full chain
  ↓ parse canonical / meta robots / X-Robots-Tag / title / h1
  ↓ extract internal hrefs → 897 further targets → fetch those too
  ↓ dedupe to 1,128 unique URLs, classify into GSC's vocabulary
```

Reproduce with [`scripts/crawl.py`](scripts/crawl.py) →
[`scripts/analyze.py`](scripts/analyze.py) →
[`scripts/worksheet.py`](scripts/worksheet.py). Standard library only, no
credentials.

## Limits of this audit

- **No Search Console API access from this environment.** Everything attributed
  to Google above comes from the three supplied exports. Google's selected
  canonical cannot be read by crawling and is not reported here for any URL.
- Per-URL lists behind each Coverage bucket were not supplied, so the 56, the
  6 canonical conflicts, the 75 `Crawled – currently not indexed` and the 14
  soft 404s are known only as counts.
- Bucket counts in `url-inventory.csv` are this crawl's classification, not
  Google's, and will not match the Coverage report exactly — the crawl reaches
  parameterised URLs Google may never have seen, and Google retains history for
  URLs no longer linked.
